**Best fit for Morvo: a specific, evidence-backed free preview → 199 SAR report → subscription, distributed first through merchant-focused content and trusted partners, with WhatsApp used for delivery and follow-up.** Do not buy broad search traffic to sell the report. At roughly **130–158 SAR contribution per report**, acquisition must cost less than that *per purchaser* before the one-off sale pays back; a safer initial operating target is **≤50–80 SAR per report buyer** until subscription retention is measured. That target is a recommendation, not a published benchmark.
